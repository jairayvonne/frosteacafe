About Frostea Café

Frostea started in 2021 as a small business with the goal of earning extra income. Originally named CakeFrost, the business eventually evolved into Frostea when we decided to focus on selling refreshing and delicious milk tea.

What began as a simple way to earn extra income grew into a passion for serving flavorful drinks and bringing happiness to our customers. Today, Frostea continues to serve every cup with dedication, quality, and love.
